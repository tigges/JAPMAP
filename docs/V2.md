# JAPMAP V2 — analysis and recommendation

Live today: [tigges.github.io/JAPMAP](https://tigges.github.io/JAPMAP/) (Phase 0 / site V7).
Design reference: [Pole to Pole artifact](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/8faa6591-a0cd-4b0f-8dd2-9a5f4c251e3c) (readable with `?chrome=none`).

This is the product brief for the next version. It is based on a full pass of the live site, the source (`src/`, `docs/CONCEPT.md`), the sibling [japanride.pages.dev](https://japanride.pages.dev/), and the Pole to Pole artifact.

---

## 1. What is live (V1 / Phase 0)

Three routes, JapanRide’s paper craft, almost no ride.

| Route | What a visitor gets |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | Hero essay, five stats, two pole cards, six stage cards with taglines |
| `/map` | Leaflet overview: eight gateway dots, straight vermillion jumps, a dashed great-circle “joke line”, EN/JA tiles |
| `/about` | Four “what this is not” notes plus a concept quote |

It does the Phase 0 job in `docs/CONCEPT.md` §8: prove the poles, name six stages, keep islands off the land line, stay legally clear of NHK. It does **not** yet do the three jobs in §3 (show the whole line, open a stage, stay with the work).

### What works

- Positioning is clear if you read: Sata and Soya are real capes; this is not the NHK catalog.
- The map is honest about the Tsugaru ferry (dashed purple).
- EN / 日本語 tiles work and persist.
- Type, paper palette, and sibling link make the two sites feel related.
- Direct `/map` and `/about` loads work on GitHub Pages via `404.html`.

### What fails as a product

These are UX problems, not missing Phase 2 data.

1. **The first screen is an argument, not a ride.** The hero leads with “one original ride” and immediately explains it against NHK. A first-time visitor cannot see the journey. JapanRide can afford that voice because it is a catalog of a TV series. JAPMAP is supposed to *be* the ride.
2. **The primary CTA is “Open the map.”** The map then says “Phase 0” and draws **straight lines between eight cities**. Straight-line hops are the thing JapanRide already taught people to distrust. The great-circle overlay exists to make a joke. On a live URL it reads as “the site is unfinished,” not as craft.
3. **Nothing is clickable.** Stage cards do not open stages. Gateway rows are `cursor: default`. There are no day pages, no filters, no shareable stage URL. Nav is Home / Map / About — a brochure, not a journey.
4. **Stats are the weakest possible numbers.** “2 mainland poles,” “8 gateways,” “~1888 km as the crow flies.” Crow-flies km is the one figure a routed ride should never lead with.
5. **The map fights the user.** `scrollWheelZoom: false` on a 72vh canvas; sidebar and map do not talk; no stage pills; legend explains a placeholder the visitor did not ask for. On a phone the map stacks above a static list — two disconnected columns.
6. **JAPANRIDE is in the chrome twice** (nav CTA + footer). It is the sibling, not the product. It should live on About / Detours, not in the header.
7. **About is written for us.** “Sibling, not a fork,” “schema,” “concept doc.” Fine for a repo. Wrong for a visitor who wanted to know how long the ride is and where they sleep.
8. **Visual language is a clone, not a sibling.** Same paper, same vermillion, same Cormorant, same uppercase eyebrows, same round cards. Next to JapanRide it looks like a reskin with the episodes stripped out — which is exactly how it was built.

CONCEPT §4 already specified `/stages`, `/days/:id`, `/practical`, `/journal`. None of that shipped. V2 should not “finish Phase 1 as more gateway dots.” It should change the *shape* of the site.

## 2. What the Pole to Pole artifact is

A **single-page, data-first story of a routed ride**, not a three-page app and not a Leaflet demo.

It loaded at `https://claude.ai/code/artifact/8faa6591-a0cd-4b0f-8dd2-9a5f4c251e3c?chrome=none` (the path without `chrome=none` is a Claude shell; unauthenticated `/api/frame` is 403).

### Shape

```
sticky stage-colour bar     3,461 km · 42 days
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Hero: split  Pole to Pole  |  Sōya monument photo
Stats:  3461 km · 44640 m · 42 days · 7 stages · 3 ferries · 489 facilities
Chart:  one bar per day (width = km, height = effort)
Stages: seven photo cards
Days:   42 rows, grouped, collapsible
Footer: how the numbers and pictures were made
```

There is **no map**. There is **no “what this is not.”** There is no JapanRide. The ride is the interface.

### The ride it describes

Kilometre **0 is Cape Sōya** (45°31′N). The pole-to-pole finish is **Cape Sata** at ~3,386 km. Stage 9 is a 76 km Satsuma run-in, Yamagawa → Kagoshima, after the southern pole — logistics, not a ninth scenic stage.

Direction: **north → south**, Sea of Japan coast, then a cut-across to Ōsaka, San’yō / Inland Sea, Kanmon, Kyūshū east coast to Sata.

Two gaps in stage numbering are ferries (Tsugaru, Kagoshima Bay). A third ferry is implied by the “3 ferries” stat (likely Kanmon or a bay hop — confirm off the GPX).

| Stage | Name | From → to | km | m+ | days |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | Hokkaidō west coast | Cape Sōya → Hakodate | 847 | 11,021 | 11 |
| 2 | *(ferry)* | Tsugaru Strait | — | — | — |
| 3 | Tōhoku, Sea of Japan | Aomori → Niigata | 557 | 7,184 | 7 |
| 4 | Hokuriku coast | Niigata → Kanazawa | 404 | 4,239 | 5 |
| 5 / 6 | Cut-across | Kanazawa → Ōsaka | 329 | 3,027 | 4 |
| 5 / 6 | Inland Sea / San’yō | Ōsaka → Shimonoseki | 646 | 6,886 | 7 |
| 7 | *(ferry)* | Kagoshima Bay (after the pole) | — | — | — |
| 8 | Kyūshū east coast | Mōji → Cape Sata | 601 | 10,958 | 7 |
| 9 | Satsuma run-in | Yamagawa → Kagoshima | 76 | 1,325 | 1 |

Stage 5 vs 6 labels in the mock are inconsistent between the card grid and the day list. V2 should number **riding stages 1–7** in geographic order and treat ferries as hops, not as missing integers.

**Effort** = km + (climbing m / 10). That single number is how 42 days become comparable. The mock’s range is about 68 (shortest) to 451 (hardest). Day 37, Usuki → Nobeoka, is the hammer: 123 km and 3,277 m.

Photography: Wikimedia Commons, credited. Only two days currently have a “milestone” dot (Day 1 Sōya, Day 32 Kure → Yanai). The rest of the grid is structured for pictures that are not there yet.

### Why it feels modern

- **Numbers a rider understands** (routed km, climbing, days), not metaphor stats.
- **One scroll** instead of three brochure pages.
- **Hierarchy:** whole ride → stages → days. You never dump 42 rows on someone without a stage.
- **A chart that is the map’s job** when you do not yet have a GPS canvas: shape of the ride at a glance.
- **Quiet chrome.** No uppercase nav of siblings. No Phase 0 apology.
- **Magazine layout:** split hero, full-bleed photos, wide whitespace, sans display type, colour as data (stage identity), not as a vermillion brand stamp on every card.

It is closer to a [Pudding](https://pudding.cool)-style scrollytelling piece, or a Komoot tour journal, than to JapanRide.

## 3. The fork in the road (do not paper over this)

`docs/CONCEPT.md` and the artifact disagree on **geography**, not only on layout.

| | CONCEPT / live V1 | Pole to Pole artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direction | South → north, spring (Sata start, Soya finish) | North → south (Sōya is km 0) |
| Stages | 6 (Kyushu, Seto/Shikoku, Kansai, Alps/Nakasendo, Tōhoku, Hokkaidō) | 7 riding stages + ferry gaps |
| Line | Interior jewels: Yamanami, **Shimanami**, Biwaichi, Nakasendo | **West coast** then San’yō: Sōya–Hakodate–Niigata–Kanazawa–Ōsaka–Shimonoseki–Sata |
| Distance | ~1,888 km crow-flies | **3,461 km routed** |
| Days | unnamed | **42 riding days**, overnight towns named |
| Islands | second act (unchanged) | off-stage (unchanged) |
| After the south pole | ride ends at Sata | Stage 9 returns to Kagoshima |

CONCEPT §11 said: *wireframe wins on layout; this brief wins on geography.* That rule was written when the wireframe was unread. We can now read it. The artifact is not a layout skin on the CONCEPT spine. It is a **different ride**, measured off a track.

**V2 recommendation: the artifact wins on both layout *and* the land line**, because it is the first version that contains an actual itinerary. Keep from CONCEPT only what still holds:

- Two mainland poles, islands off the land line, no 日本四極 product yet.
- Honest ferry/train styling; never stroke a boat as a road.
- No NHK stills; JapanRide remains the catalog sibling.
- Official cycle routes as an underlay later, not the story.

If the GPX behind the artifact is the line you intend to ride, do not keep selling Shimanami as a default stage. It can stay a detour (and a JapanRide link). If you still want the CONCEPT scenic spine instead, the artifact is the wrong reference — say so and we restyle V1 without adopting Sōya-as-km-0.

Assume from here that **V2 is the routed Sōya → Sata west-coast ride**, told with the artifact’s information design, plus a real map the mock omitted.

## 4. Product thesis for V2

> One road down the length of Japan. Forty-two days, measured off the track. Open any day.

Three jobs, restated for a visitor (not for the repo):

1. **See the whole ride in five seconds** — colour bar + km + days. Sōya on the left, Sata on the right (or top-to-bottom on a phone).
2. **Pick a stage, then a day** — photo, km, climbing, effort, overnight. No essay required.
3. **Put that day on a map** — real geometry when we have it; gateway hops until we do. EN/JA tiles stay.

Out of scope for V2 (same as CONCEPT): live GPS, booking, Strava import, social feed, four poles, island packages as stages.

In scope that CONCEPT deferred too far: **photos, a day list, and numbers from the track.** Those are what make the site feel finished. Phase-2 “thicken two pilot stages into GPS” can follow immediately after.

## 5. Information architecture

Keep shareable URLs. Prefer **one scrolling home** that *is* the journey, with deep links into the same content.

```
/                         Story: hero, stats, chart, stages, days
/map                      Full-line map (optional: ?day=37&stage=kyushu)
/stages/:id               Stage — photo, totals, day list, local map
/days/:id                 Day — from/to, km, m+, effort, notes, map
/about                    What this is, sibling, licences, method
```

Drop `/` vs `/map` vs `/about` as equal top-level ideas. Nav becomes:

**Ride · Map · About**

- **Ride** is the default (today’s Home, rebuilt).
- **Map** is a tool, not the product.
- **About** holds JapanRide, Wikimedia credits, how km were measured, “not NHK.”

Do not ship empty `/journal` or `/practical` in V2 unless there is real content. A “Practical” block on About (season, Tsugaru ferry, Sata is a dead-end so Stage 9 exists) is enough.

Hash targets on `/` (`#chart`, `#stage-hokkaido`, `#day-37`) so the sticky bar can jump without a router round-trip.

## 6. Screens

### 6.1 Ride (home)

**Sticky bar.** Seven colour segments, width ∝ stage km. Right: `3,461 km · 42 days`. **Make it clickable** (the mock does not). Click scrolls to that stage and highlights the chart.

**Hero.** Split: left type, right one photograph of a pole.

- Eyebrow: `Cape Sōya → Cape Sata · by bicycle` (and 宗谷岬 → 佐多岬).
- Title: **Pole to Pole** — not “one island chain / one original ride / two poles.”
- One sentence of body, then the two coordinates: Sōya `45°31′N · km 0`, Sata `30°59′N · km 3,461`.
- No primary button to a blank map. Secondary text link: `Open the map` under the stats, once the visitor has seen the ride.

**Stats strip.** Six figures from the track, not from the metaphor:

| Show | Do not show |
| --- | --- |
| 3,461 km routed | 1,888 km crow-flies |
| 44,640 m climbing | “2 mainland poles” |
| 42 riding days | “8 gateways” |
| 7 stages | |
| 3 ferries | |
| 489 facilities *(only if we can name the source)* | |

If the facilities count cannot be reproduced from open data, drop it rather than inventing a fourth kind of honesty problem.

**Chart.** One bar per riding day. Width = km, height = effort. Hatched bars or gaps for ferry days. Hover/focus: `Day 32 · Kure → Yanai · 101.9 km · 1,497 m · effort 252`. Click opens `/days/32` or expands that row.

**Seven stage cards.** Photo + colour pip + name + from/to + km / m+ / days. The whole card is a link to `/stages/:id`. Caption under the image, Wikimedia credit required.

**Every day.** Stages as `<details>` (first stage open, rest closed on desktop; all closed on small screens except the one in view). Each row: day index, from → to, km, climbing, effort bar. Filled dot if that day has photography. Row is a link.

**Footer method.** Three short columns from the mock (numbers, effort, pictures). This is better About-copy than “sibling, not a fork.”

### 6.2 Map

A **full-viewport tool**, not a chapter under an essay.

- Default: whole Sōya–Sata line. Stage colour on the stroke.
- Click a stage in the sticky bar or a sidebar row → fit bounds, open that stage’s days.
- Click a day → zoom, highlight, show km / m+ / overnight.
- Layers, when they exist: `Our line` (on) · `Official network` (off) · `NHK detours` (off).
- EN / 日本語 stays.
- **Turn scroll-wheel zoom on** for the map page (keep it off only if the map is embedded mid-scroll on `/`).
- Delete the great-circle joke from the default view. Keep it as a one-line toggle “Show straight-line distance” if you still want the teaching moment.
- Mobile: map 55–60vh, day list in a sheet that peeks 8rem and expands.

Until GPX exists, draw **great-circle-per-day** between overnight towns (42 short hops), not 8 stage-gateway jumps. That already looks like a ride. Label it `planned overnights — not the GPS yet`.

### 6.3 Stage

Kana, colour, photo, the three totals, why this coast exists (one paragraph, people and weather, not NHK episode titles), then the day list and a cropped map. Link JapanRide only where an itinerary actually touches (e.g. Aomori, Noto/Kanazawa, Onomichi, Ōita/Miyazaki).

### 6.4 Day

From → to, km, climbing, effort, high point if we have it, overnight town, ferry/train callout, photo slot (empty state: “Photograph when we have one,” never a stock image without a caption). Map at the day’s bounds. Prev / next day.

### 6.5 About

Rewrite for humans:

1. This is an original bicycle traverse, pole to pole.
2. It is not *Cycle Around Japan*; watch that on JapanRide.
3. How distance and effort were measured.
4. Photo licences.
5. Islands are extra; Sata is a dead-end, which is why the last day returns to Kagoshima.

## 7. Visual language

Stay in the **family** of JapanRide so a shared visitor is not lost. Stop cloning the brochure.

**Keep**

- Vermillion as the one accent (`#c4452d`) — use it for “to” in the title, the active day, links.
- Ink and a warm paper ground, slightly closer to white than `#f3eee4` so photographs pop.
- Zen Kaku Gothic New for JA and UI.
- Round-rect map shell when the map appears.

**Change**

- **Display type:** a sharp grotesque for “Pole to Pole” and numbers (the mock’s magazine headline), not Cormorant on every heading. Keep Cormorant only for long quotes, if at all.
- **Stage colour is data.** The chart’s blue→green→tan→pink→orange→red sequence is the way you recognise where you are. Do not paint every card with a 4px vermillion top border.
- **Photography is the texture**, not a CSS gradient veil. Hero is a real pole. Stage cards are real places. Never an NHK still.
- **Less uppercase tracking.** Eyebrows can stay small; body should look like a page, not a pitch deck.
- **Dark footer optional.** A light method footer (as in the mock) is easier to read and matches the piece.

Motion: short, local. Sticky bar highlight, chart tooltip, `details` open. No hero parallax, no map fly-to on page load.

## 8. Interaction details that make it “user friendly”

These are the concrete upgrades V1 is missing.

| Problem now | V2 |
| --- | --- |
| Three pages, equal weight | One story + a map tool |
| CTA to an empty map | Scroll into the ride; map is opt-in |
| Static gateway list | Every row is a day you can open |
| Crow-flies km | Track km + climbing + effort |
| “Phase 0” copy on the map | Honest label only where geometry is still schematic |
| Wheel zoom disabled | Enabled on `/map` |
| JAPANRIDE in the header | JapanRide on About / detour chips only |
| No photos | Wikimedia, credited, with empty slots |
| No keyboard story | Chart and day list are focusable; `←` `→` between days |
| 404 status on `/map` | Keep `404.html`; add a visible “Ride / Map / About” so people do not land lost |
| Mobile nav wraps into a second row | Single row: wordmark, Ride, Map, overflow for About |

Internationalisation: do not wait for a full i18n framework. Ship **bilingual labels** (place names + kana) as now, English narrative first, 日本語 as a second pass on the Ride page. Map tiles already switch.

Accessibility: the mock’s colour-only stages fail without names. Every bar needs a text alternative (`aria-label="Day 32, Kure to Yanai, 102 km"`). Do not rely on tooltip-only.

## 9. Data

Keep layers unmerged (`schema.ts`). Add a **ride file** that the UI actually uses.

```ts
type Ride = {
  id: "soya-sata";
  direction: "north-to-south";
  km: number;          // 3461
  climbM: number;      // 44640
  days: RideDay[];     // 42
  stages: RideStage[]; // 7 riding + ferry hops
};

type RideDay = {
  n: number;           // 1…42
  from: string;
  to: string;
  fromJa: string;
  toJa: string;
  km: number;
  climbM: number;
  effort: number;      // km + climbM / 10
  stageId: string;
  ferryAfter?: boolean;
  photo?: { src: string; credit: string; licence: string };
};
```

**Source of truth:** the GPX / route file the artifact was measured from. Transcribing the 42 overnights from the mock is a start; it is not a substitute for the track. Until the file is in the repo:

- Days and stage totals can be hardcoded from the artifact (they already sum to ~3,460 km).
- Map geometry = overnight coordinates (geocode once, store as data).
- Do not display “489 facilities” until the POI source is in-tree.

Tests to add: day count = 42; Sōya is north of Sata; each day’s effort = km + climb/10; stage km sums to ride km within 1 km; ferries are not counted as riding days; Stage 9 starts after Sata.

The live V1 tests that **force** `direction: "south-to-north"` and six CONCEPT stages will have to change. That is intentional.

## 10. Build plan

Same stack (Vite, React, TypeScript, Leaflet, Vitest, GitHub Pages `dist`). No new framework.

### V2.0 — Story site (this is the version that should replace what’s live)

- Rebuild `/` as the artifact: sticky bar, split hero, stats, day chart, stage cards, day list, method footer.
- 42 days as data; Wikimedia photos for hero + seven stages (licence in the caption).
- `/about` rewritten; JapanRide removed from the header.
- `/map` kept, but driven by overnight towns, scroll-wheel on, no joke line by default.
- Stage colour tokens shared by bar, chart, cards, map stroke.

This can ship without GPX. It will already feel like a different product.

### V2.1 — The map catches up

- Fit from a day; sticky bar linked to the map.
- `/stages/:id` and `/days/:id` as real routes (home can still scroll).
- Geocoded overnights; ferry hops styled dashed.

### V2.2 — The track

- Import GPX / GeoJSON; replace schematic hops with the measured line.
- Elevation for the chart’s “high point.”
- Optional official-network teal underlay on days that actually use one.

### V2.3 — Detours and practical

- Thin chips to JapanRide where an NHK itinerary touches the line.
- Practical: when to start if you reverse the story (southbound vs northbound), Tsugaru ferry, Sata dead-end, seasonal notes.
- Journal remains empty until there are notes. No fake entries.

Do not wait for V2.2 to replace V1. The live site’s problem is the **interface**, not only the missing GPS.

## 11. What not to do

- Do not reskin V1 with larger type and call it V2.
- Do not put a dashed great-circle on the homepage.
- Do not lead with NHK, “sibling, not a fork,” or “Phase 0.”
- Do not invent GPX, facilities, or journal entries.
- Do not merge Shimanami into this west-coast line just because CONCEPT liked it.
- Do not keep Sata → Soya as the canonical direction if the track is Sōya → Sata. Offer reverse as a view later.
- Do not load JapanRide’s VOD grid or stills.
- Do not add auth, accounts, or a route planner.

---

## 12. Success look

A first-time visitor, on a phone, without reading a README, can answer:

1. This is a bicycle ride from Cape Sōya to Cape Sata.
2. It is about 3,500 km and 42 days, on a west-coast line with a few ferries.
3. They can tap a stage and see the overnight towns.
4. They can open a map if they want geography; they did not have to.
5. This is not the NHK series — they learn that on About, not in the headline.

When that is true, thicken the line with GPX. Not before.
